How we handle an incident

  1. DetectAlerts reach the right person, not everyone.
  2. TriageWe confirm impact and set severity within minutes of pickup.
  3. MitigateRestore service first: rollback, scale or failover.
  4. CommunicateRegular updates to your team until resolved.
  5. LearnPostmortem, permanent fix and a better runbook.
